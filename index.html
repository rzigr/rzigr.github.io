Hi, I'm Igor, a PhD student at the Department of Computing Science, Umeå University.

  • I am part of the Foundations of Language Processing research group and belong to the WASP-HS graduate school
  • My advisors are Johanna Björklund (Umeå University) and Carl Öhman (Uppsala University)
  • Until June 2025, I am a visiting PhD student at the NLPNorth (IT University of Copenhagen), wokring with Christian Hardmeier
  • I have a Master's degree in Data Science (University of Gothenburg) and Bachelor's degree in Business Informatics (Higher School of Economics)

I am defending in spring 2026 and looking for a postdoc position. Let me know if you hear of something relevant!

My main reaseach area is computational media studies, and various related tasks such as narrative, sentiment and stance analysis. Semantic changes between domains and publications is also something I find fascinating. I am curious (yet remain unconvinced) about computational fact-checking and fake news detection. More broadly, I'm intersted in most things involving NLP methods as part of mixed-method approaches in computational social sciences. In my thesis work, I am focusing on narratives that manipulate agency (for instance, to antropomorphise technology or dehumanise people) and the linguistic devices that enable them.
